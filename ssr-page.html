What this measures — and why it matters

The WHO-5 is one of the most widely used wellbeing measures in the world — a low team score is an early-warning signal that often shows up later as medical claims and absence.

What moves this score

Wellbeing scores respond best to layered support: skills are introduced, then practiced daily until they become habit — behavior research shows roughly two weeks of repetition is what makes a new skill stick (Lally, 2010).

What this measures — and why it matters

Chronic stress is the quiet budget line: stressed employees lose roughly 27 productive workdays per year (Kessler, Harvard), and unmanaged stress escalates into ER visits and admissions — mindfulness-based programs cut emergency utilization by up to 50% (Kaiser Permanente).

What moves this score

Your team's stress is above the typical range — presenteeism is likely already costing more than a program would.

The strongest evidence for reducing workplace stress comes from structured, mindfulness-based group training — the same clinical approach that cut healthcare utilization by 43% at Mass General.

What this measures — and why it matters

Engagement is the sub-score most tightly linked to performance and retention (Harter/Gallup) — and emotional-intelligence skills explain up to 67% of the variance in leadership effectiveness (Goleman).

What moves this score

Engagement grows when people gain emotional-intelligence skills and see their leaders model them — leadership behavior explains much of the variance in team effectiveness (Goleman; Harter/Gallup).

What this measures — and why it matters

Loneliness at work is the most under-measured driver of turnover and disengagement — most companies have never assessed it.

What moves this score

Your team's connection score is below typical — the most common finding in hybrid and remote teams, and one of the most fixable.

Connection is built through structured shared experience — teams that practice together, especially across remote and hybrid distance, rebuild belonging measurably.
